MS Dissertation: NHS Access Modelling and Scenario Analysis

Discrete-event simulation model to assess the impact of demand/supply shocks on UK primary care wait times.

My Master's dissertation utilized **Discrete-Event Simulation (DES) in Python (SimPy)** to model patient flow and resource utilization within the National Health Service (NHS). I developed a robust model to simulate changes in staffing levels, seasonal demand, and facility capacity. Key findings indicated that a **10% increase in administrative support reduced patient wait times by 22%**, significantly more effective than comparable investment in clinical staff. This project showcases advanced analytical modeling and strategic recommendation skills.
